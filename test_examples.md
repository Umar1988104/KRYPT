# Curated Test Examples

Use these for practice runs before the demo, and to have known-good examples
ready if judges ask to see specific categories. Test each one 2-3 times before
the actual demo/video recording so you know the app's real behaviour.

## Phishing (expected: Phishing, high confidence)

1. "Dear Customer, your SBI account will be BLOCKED within 24 hours due to
   incomplete KYC. Update immediately: http://sbi-kyc-update.in/verify"

2. "Aapka parcel customs mein hold hai. 49 rupay pay karke release karwayein:
   bit.ly/parcel-release-49"

3. "CONGRATULATIONS! You have WON Rs 25,00,000 in KBC Lucky Draw. Send your
   bank details to claim: kbc-lottery-winner@gmail.com"

4. "This is Delhi Police Cyber Cell. Your Aadhaar is linked to illegal
   activity. You are under DIGITAL ARREST. Do not disconnect. Pay fine of
   Rs 50,000 immediately via UPI to avoid legal action."

## Suspicious (expected: Suspicious, medium confidence — judgement call cases)

5. "Hi, this is HR from [a real-sounding company]. We have a work-from-home
   job for you, Rs 30,000/month, no experience needed. Registration fee Rs 500."

## Safe (expected: Safe, high confidence)

6. "Hi, this is a reminder that your electricity bill for August is due on
   the 5th. You can pay it on the official portal or at any authorized centre."

7. "Your Amazon order #402-1234567 has been shipped and will arrive by
   Thursday. Track it in the Amazon app."

8. "Reminder: Your college fee installment is due on 15th September. Please
   pay via the official student portal."

## How to record confidence numbers for your PPT

Run all 8 examples, note down the verdict + confidence for each, and use that
as your honest "tested against N examples" line in the Feasibility slide —
don't invent an accuracy percentage.
